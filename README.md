One line roasting an alarm clock that rings at 6 AM on a Saturday, written
for the [Snooze Button Roast](https://make.fun/c/u-carbon-forge-snooze-button-roast-14cbafc6)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt` — a single line of at most
200 characters.

## The roast

> My alarm rang at 6 AM on a Saturday with the confidence of a man who has never once checked a calendar — so I made the snooze button my union rep.
